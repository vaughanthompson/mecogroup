How We Work

We place a strong emphasis on total management. As a hub between you and our team of assesors, designers and trades- Meco's process is designed to effortlessly manage the organisational load of multiple projects, timelines and scale.

 
 
 
 

Line-by-line transparent quoting


Itemised costs for materials, labour and margins ensure clients can be confident in that they're receiving fair value- a key value of Meco Group. For larger multi-unit clients this also enables managers to easily collate cost data points for more intricate analysis, aiding future decision making.

LBP sign-off on restricted building work (RBW)


We ensure all critical works (structural, weathertightness, fire safety) comply in design and contruction with the New Zealand Building Code. The documentation for any RBW is duly formalised and upon completion for lodging with local council for Code Compliance Certification & attached to the property's public record.

Minimal disruption in occupied buildings


All works are carried out with complete consideration for any owners, tenants or client employees. Not only do we ensure clear communication, respect and friendly interactions- we also strive to have projects completed end-to-end as quickly as possible to minimise disruption on site.

After-hours and weekend availability


You can't control when things break- or when accidental damage occurs. Whether man-made, environmental or mechanical, we have an on-call team available to 'make-safe' or repair the issue when you need us.

Owner-ready reporting and stakeholder communication


We understand there is a difference in the communication needs of a single-dwelling occupier and a property manager with a number of sites under their supervision. Owner occupiers need micro updates and the ability to be involved as the work progresses. With large scale relationships - we often filter these comms down to KPI-driven or time-based reporting across the entire portfolio. This reduces noise but ensures managers can monitor the overarching situation as well as prepare accurate reporting within their organisation.

Delivery to agreed KPIs and SLAs


Our experience straddling construction and assessment from both supplier and client viewpoints positions Meco Group to seamlessly integrate with large scale multi-unit management entities. We know how to negotiate, set and perform to realistic key performance indicators and comprehensively prepared service level agreements that give clients the peace of mind to trust Meco Group implicitly.
