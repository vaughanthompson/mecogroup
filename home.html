Construction? Yes. Project Management? Absolutely. But Meco is so much more.


Formerly Jordent Construction, Meco Group has evolved into a entity where construction is merely one of many services we provide. Operating at scale NZ-wide our systems are built to move high volumes of work efficiently, carrying the reporting discipline and communication standards we developed into every job.



Case Studies How We Work

When we say no job too big or too small. We mean it.


From a $2,000 make-safe to a half-million-dollar fire rebuild, Meco manages the whole process across residential and light commercial property - assessment, scoping, reinstatement and sign-off - through a single point of contact. We deliver on every job, whatever its size.
